Privacy policy

Last updated: [date of approval]

This policy explains what personal information Kindness In Action UK collects when you donate or use this website, why we collect it, and what your rights are. We try to collect as little as we need.

Who we are

Kindness In Action UK is a registered charity in England and Wales, number 1219528. We are the “controller” of the personal information described here, and we are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) as a data controller. Our address is Unit 5A, Honeypot Business Centre, Parr Road, Stanmore HA7 1NL. You can contact us about anything in this policy at contact@kiauk.org.

What we collect

When you donate through this website we collect:

  • your first name, last name and email address;
  • if you give as a business or organisation, its name;
  • the amount, date and frequency of your donation, and whether it was paid;
  • if you choose Gift Aid, your home address (house name or number and postcode are required, street and town are optional), the wording of the declaration you agreed to, and the date you made it;
  • whether you agreed to receive updates from us, and when;
  • if you give as a business, whether you are happy for us to thank you publicly.

We do not see or store your card number or bank details. These are entered on a page run by Stripe, our payment provider, which keeps them under its own privacy policy.

If you email us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

What we doLegal basis
Take your donation, send you a receipt and keep a record of itOur legitimate interests in running the charity and accepting gifts, and our legal duty to keep accounting records
Claim Gift Aid from HMRC and keep the records HMRC requiresLegal obligation, and your declaration

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for automated decisions or profiling.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of providers to run the donation service. They act on our instructions and may only use your information for that purpose.

  • Stripe takes payments and sends receipts. Stripe also acts as a controller of the payment information you give it, for fraud prevention and its own legal duties.
  • Supabase hosts the database where we keep donation and Gift Aid records. The database is located in the United Kingdom (London).
  • GitHub hosts this website. It may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Microsoft provides our email.

We share Gift Aid details (your name, home address, donation date and amount) with HMRC when we make a claim. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Some of these providers are based in, or may access data from, countries outside the UK, including the United States. Where this happens we rely on approved safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

  • Donation and Gift Aid records: for at least six years after the end of the financial year the donation relates to, because HMRC and charity accounting rules require it.
  • Marketing preferences: until you unsubscribe or ask us to stop.
  • Emails to us: for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and then no more than two years unless there is a reason to keep them.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Stripe’s payment page may set cookies it needs to take payment and prevent fraud. See Stripe’s privacy policy for details.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law (for example Gift Aid records);
  • stop using your information for marketing, or for any other use based on your consent;
  • cancel a Gift Aid declaration, which stops us claiming on future donations.

Email contact@kiauk.org and we will reply within one month. If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We would like the chance to put things right first.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
