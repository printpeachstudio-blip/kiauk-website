Who we are

Kindness In Action UK grew out of Organising Food For London’s Homeless (OFFL). Three people started it in May 2012 to feed and support people sleeping rough in Central London, and all three are still involved.

The outreach has run every week since. Volunteers hand out hot food, clothing, toiletries and seasonal essentials, give practical advice and point people to specialist services. You don’t need to be assessed to get basic support.

The Charity Commission has recently authorised us as a Charitable Incorporated Organisation. Our registered charity number is 1219528.

Where we are heading

Keep the Central London outreach running at the standard people rely on.

Open more outreach and partnerships, growing carefully as resources allow.

Give out more food, clothing, toiletries and seasonal essentials, and build better routes from a hot meal to specialist help.

Our vision

An end to hunger and homelessness in our lifetime.

Our mission

To bring practical help, human connection and dignity to people facing homelessness, poverty and vulnerability, through consistent frontline action and community partnership.

What we do

Our main programme is the weekly Central London outreach. We don’t ask people to be assessed first, so we can help quickly.

  • Hot food

    Meals prepared and packed in sealed containers in a kitchen with a five-star food hygiene rating.

  • Clothing and essentials

    Clothing, toiletries and seasonal essentials, including winter packs of warm clothing.

  • Surplus food

    Donated and surplus food passed on to partner foodbanks and soup kitchens.

  • Advice and referrals

    Practical advice, and referrals to homelessness, health, welfare and other specialist services.

We also support partner organisations in London and other parts of England. Our outreach partners are the Missionaries of Charity in south-east, west and north-west London and in Liverpool. Our foodbank partners include Truswell and London Community Kitchen. The Jalaram Mandir in Greenford provides the facilities and prepares the hot meals we serve.

How we work

We turn up. Volunteers are there week after week.

We keep it practical. Help without unnecessary bureaucracy.

We treat people with respect. Everyone is dealt with professionally and with dignity.

We respond fast. When a new need appears, we act on it.

We have no overheads. Volunteers do the work, so resources go to frontline activity.

We pass things on responsibly. Donated and surplus food and goods go where they are needed.

We stay. Our founders and many volunteers have been involved for years.

We keep boundaries. We build trust with the people we see regularly while staying professional.

Some examples of how we made a difference

A hot meal and a winter pack

One very cold week, a man came to the outreach for the first time. He told volunteers he had not had a proper hot meal for four days and had nothing left, not even a change of clothes. He went away with a hot meal and a winter pack of warm clothing and toiletries.

A women’s refuge ran out

A refuge had used up its food and funding with more than 100 women and children due the next day. We gave every family food for breakfast, lunch and dinner, and kept topping up its foodbank afterwards.

Elderly residents who could not get to the foodbank

Many could not reach it before stock ran out. We arranged extra supplies and delivered essentials to their homes.

Safe delivery

Volunteers work to agreed standards. Our meals are prepared and packed in sealed containers in a kitchen that has consistently held a five-star food hygiene rating.

Volunteer induction and operating standards have changed over the years as food safety rules and the needs of the people we help have changed. We aim to work with partners that are registered charities with similar objectives.

Who runs us

Our constitution has been adopted and our trustees appointed. The five trustees include OFFL’s founders, other experienced volunteers, and people with professional backgrounds in business, finance, healthcare, charity governance, HR and law.

Between them they have worked with government, regulators, small businesses and large companies.
