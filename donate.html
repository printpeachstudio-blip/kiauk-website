Winter Bag Appeal 2026

£40

sponsors one winter bag

Each bag holds 19 winter essentials, including outerwear and waterproofs, thermal layers and toiletries. We want to fill 600 bags this winter.

Bags are handed out in person from mid-December, at our own outreach and through partner outreach programmes in London and Liverpool.

Local businesses can sponsor 10, 25 or 50 bags as Friend, Supporter or Champion, or any number from one. Questions? Email contact@kiauk.org.
