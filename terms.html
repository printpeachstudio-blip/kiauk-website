Donation terms

Last updated: [date of approval]

These terms apply to gifts made through this website to Kindness In Action UK, registered charity number 1219528 (“we”, “us”). By making a donation you agree to them.

How your gift is used

Gifts are used for our charitable purposes: our weekly outreach in Central London, food, clothing and practical help for people facing homelessness and hunger, and related work.

Gifts made for the Winter Bag Appeal (for example in multiples of £40) will be spent on winter bags and their contents. If the appeal raises more than we can use on bags this winter, or the appeal cannot go ahead as planned, we will use the surplus for our other charitable work. Bags go out from mid-December, in person, at our outreach and through partner outreach programmes.

Making a donation

Gifts are taken in pounds sterling by Stripe, our payment provider. The smallest gift we accept online is £1 and the largest is £10,000. For larger gifts please email contact@kiauk.org. We will email a receipt to the address you give. Please check it is right.

Monthly gifts

A monthly gift is taken on the same day each month until you cancel it. To cancel or change a monthly gift, email contact@kiauk.org from the address you used to give. We will stop it before the next payment where we can, and in any case within five working days.

Refunds

We will refund a gift if you ask within 14 days of making it, or if you gave by mistake. Email contact@kiauk.org with the email address you gave from and the date. If we have already claimed Gift Aid on a refunded gift, we will repay it to HMRC.

Gift Aid

Gift Aid is available to UK taxpayers giving as individuals. If you tick the Gift Aid box, you confirm the declaration shown on the donation page. It covers the gift you are making, any gifts you make to us in the future, and gifts you have made in the past four years, until you cancel it.

You must pay at least as much Income Tax and/or Capital Gains Tax in the tax year as the amount of Gift Aid claimed on all your donations to all charities. If you pay less, you are responsible for paying the difference. Council Tax and VAT do not qualify. Gift Aid cannot be claimed on gifts made by limited companies or other organisations, or on payments you receive something in return for.

To cancel your declaration, or to tell us about a change of name or home address, email contact@kiauk.org.

Business gifts

Limited companies cannot claim Gift Aid, but may be able to deduct donations to a registered charity from taxable profits for Corporation Tax. Sole traders and partners giving from their own money can give as individuals with Gift Aid. We recommend you check your own tax position. We cannot give tax advice.

Giving a gift does not give you any right to control how we run our work. If you agree to be thanked publicly, we may name your business on our website and social media. Email us if you change your mind and we will remove it.

Your information
